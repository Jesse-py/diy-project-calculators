How deep should topsoil be?

  • New lawn: 4 to 6 inches. Penn State Extension recommends a minimum of 4 to 6 inches (firmed or settled depth) of good-quality topsoil for good turf, and suggests working about 2 inches of it into the top 2 to 3 inches of the subsoil underneath so the layers blend. If you're adding several inches, lower the subgrade first so the finished lawn meets paths and doorways at the right height.
  • Raised vegetable beds: 8 to 12 inches when the ground below is poor. Colorado State University Extension says that where the soil below isn't suitable for crop growth, 8 to 12 inches of soil is considered minimal. If roots can grow down into decent soil under the bed, a lower bed works too.
  • Let it settle before you seed. Penn State notes that rain or irrigation can settle new soil instead of rolling it; spread a little extra, water it, and rake it level before seeding or sodding.

How the calculator works

Volume is area × depth, with the depth converted from inches to feet. A 20 × 10 ft area is 200 square feet; at 4 inches that's 200 × 4 ÷ 12 = 66.7 cubic feet. Adding 10% for settling gives 73.3 cubic feet. Divide by 27 for cubic yards (2.72) or by the bag size for bags (74 bags of 1 cubic foot). Bags are rounded up. For a circle, the area is π × radius².

FAQ

How many bags of topsoil are in a yard?

A cubic yard is 27 cubic feet: 36 bags of 0.75 cubic feet, 27 bags of 1 cubic foot, 18 bags of 1.5 cubic feet, or 13.5 bags of 2 cubic feet.

How much topsoil do I need for 1,000 square feet?

At 4 inches, 12.35 cubic yards; at 6 inches, 18.52 cubic yards. Add your settling allowance on top (10% makes those about 13.6 and 20.4 yards).

How much soil for a 4 × 8 raised bed?

At 10 inches deep, 26.7 cubic feet, or just under 1 cubic yard: 18 bags of 1.5 cubic feet or 14 bags of 2 cubic feet. At 12 inches it's 32 cubic feet.

Topsoil, garden soil, or raised-bed mix?

The volume math is the same for all of them. Topsoil is for filling and grading lawns and beds; bagged garden soil and raised-bed mixes usually have compost or other organic matter mixed in. Check the bag for what's in it.

How much does a yard of topsoil weigh?

It depends heavily on how wet the soil is and what's in it, so ask your supplier before you plan to haul it in a pickup. This calculator works in volume, which is how topsoil is sold. For stone sold by the ton, use the gravel calculator.
